# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

> Inferred from the explicit agency brief and the user's checkpoint answers (no separate init interview: the brief already answered users, purpose and constraints, and the user validated a content summary before build).

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML + CSS + vanilla JS with Tailwind CSS via CDN. No build step, no bundler. Deploy as static files (GitHub Pages, Netlify, Vercel static or any host).

## Users

People in Santo André, SP who need glasswork for an apartment or house (box, mirrors, balcony closures, railings, covers, windows) and compare vidraçarias before asking for a quote. They arrive from Google, Instagram or a WhatsApp link, usually on a phone, and want proof that the work is clean and delivered on time.

## Product Purpose

Santo Vidro is a vidraçaria (glass shop) in Santo André, SP. The site exists to turn a visitor into a WhatsApp quote request within seconds. Success is a click on a WhatsApp CTA (tracked with GoatCounter events).

## Positioning

Real projects, real Google reviews (4,8 with 55 ratings), a team that delivers on schedule. A neighbouring shop cannot truthfully copy these reviews or this portfolio.

## Operating Context

All conversion happens on WhatsApp (11) 93029-7232. Quotes are free-form and price-less on the site (no prices are published). Physical address: R. Rio Grande do Sul, 40, Cidade São Jorge, Santo André, SP, 09112-200.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services: Espelhos, Box, Sacadas, Portas e Janelas, Guarda-corpo, Coberturas, Fechamentos.
- Experience claims chosen by the user: mais de 15 anos de mercado, mais de 15 mil clientes atendidos (owner's Google reply).
- Missing, must NOT be invented: e-mail, full opening hours (Google only says "abre às 09:00"), prices, "Santo Vídeo" content, blog post bodies.
- The "oferta" section was adapted from "sessão pontual / plano 90 / plano 180" to three ways of hiring without prices (user approved).
- Analytics: GoatCounter only (https://teocode.goatcounter.com/count). No Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the exact original logo file (`assets/logo-santo-vidro.png`, from santovidro.com.br). Never redraw or restyle it.
- Keep the original palette: logo teal #1CB2A9 and navy #022946 from the site's menu.
- Agency signature in the footer: "Site desenvolvido por TeoCode · © 2026 TeoCode" linking to https://teocode.com.br/.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real project photos from the client's own site (`assets/img/`), converted to WebP.
- Six real Google reviews (selected by recency among positive ones), 4,8 average, 55 ratings.
- Social: Instagram @santovidro, Facebook santovidroexcelencia, Google Business profile.
- No team photos, no videos, no partner logos cleared for use.

## Product Principles

1. Every CTA is one tap to WhatsApp with a message that says which section the visitor came from.
2. Photos of finished work carry the page; copy stays short and factual.
3. Nothing on the page that the client cannot back up (reviews link to Google, numbers come from the owner).
4. A tracking failure never breaks a link.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, visible focus, alt text on every image, reduced-motion respected, keyboard-operable menu and gallery, Portuguese (pt-BR) throughout.
